## Visual direction now treated as current

- Light grey / satin body rather than the earlier dark interface.
- POD A–F are visibly separated and retain restrained identity accents.
- Large waveform is the dominant interaction surface.
- Focused POD view uses the same hierarchy as the six-POD overview, only expanded.
- Six Gen Modes remain directly visible: PURE, JUNG, WEIGHTED, WALK, MEMORY, CHAOS.
- Random/Jung, Jitter and RND2 are large horizontal performance controls.
- Three-band EQ uses dedicated knobs.
- Master transport and global controls are consolidated into the lower master strip.
- BPM and Tap sit next to MASTER.
- Swing, MIDI Clock Out and output metering live in the master area rather than inside individual PODs.
