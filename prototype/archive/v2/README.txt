flöde~ prototype 02

This iteration focuses on presentation quality rather than adding more features.

Changes:
- larger, calmer Simplr-like waveform screens
- draggable loop start/end handles
- clickable transient markers
- cleaner typography and hierarchy
- restrained matte Amiga/MPC grey palette
- clearer master FX chain
- all previously confirmed controls retained
- no reactive UI wobble or flashy modulation animation

Interaction:
- drop audio directly on a waveform
- drag amber loop handles
- click transient markers
- SHIFT + drag for fine control
- CTRL + wheel over waveform to zoom
- move/resize BPM window
- click BPM value, type a number, Enter
